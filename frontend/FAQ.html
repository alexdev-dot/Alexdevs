Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with me.

What services do you offer?

I specialize in building high-performance, responsive websites and dynamic web applications. My services include Full Stack Development, UI/UX Design conversions to code, API integrations, and ongoing website maintenance.

What technologies do you use?

I strictly adhere to modern standards. My core stack involves HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), React.js for frontend, and Node.js for backend. I also utilize tools like Git, GitHub, and Figma for workflow efficiency.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is unique. I offer tiered packages starting from affordable landing pages to comprehensive enterprise solutions. Please check the pricing section on the home page or contact me directly for a custom quote based on your specific requirements.

How long will it take to complete my website?

A standard informational website typically takes 1-2 weeks. More complex applications with databases and user authentication can take 4-6 weeks. I provide a detailed timeline upon project kick-off.

Do you offer support after the site is launched?

Yes! I believe in long-term partnerships. I offer 24/7 support for critical issues and have maintenance packages to keep your site secure, updated, and running smoothly week after week.

Can you update or fix my existing website?

Absolutely. I can perform a comprehensive audit of your current site to identify performance bottlenecks or design issues, and then implement the necessary upgrades to bring it up to modern standards.
